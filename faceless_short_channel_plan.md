## 4. Audio Strategy

You asked: *existing copyright-free audio OR create your own.* Both.

### Path A — Copyright-free library (recommended for start)
| Source | License | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube Audio Library | No copyright, monetizable | Best category/volume/filter search |
| Pixabay Music | Royalty-free | Large Indian-exotic collection |
| Free Music Archive | CC-by/CC0 mix | Check per-track |
| Incompetech / Kevin MacLeod | CC-BY (attribution req.) | Never paywalled |

**Best sub-sets for this niche:** "cinematic," "soft strings," "piano ambient," "Sitar," "Kerala flute," "Tabla," "Fusion folk."

**Pillar→audio matching:**
| Pillar | Use |
|---|---|
| Cinema | Low strings, brass swell, suspense |
| Spiritual | Sitar, kalimba, piano, empty-hall reverb |
| Automobile | Driving pulse, bass, rhythmic percussion |
| Cooking | ASMR pan/cutlery, warm ambient |
| AI Education | Clean synth, minimalist piano |
| Investments | Quiet acoustic, steady rhythm |
| Language/Culture | Folk, flute, bhavgeete |

**Rule:** Never loop a 2–3s riff endlessly. Keep a fade in/out. Never mix two loud sources. Keep master level around −14 LUFS (YouTube standard).

### Path B — Custom AI music (build a branded sound)
- **Suno AI** (fast, high quality, good Indian-music crossover):
  - Prompt examples: `"Cinematic orchestral bhajan, 1970s Indian fusion, sitar + strings, awe-inspiring, minor to major lift, male tenor, slow build"`; `"Kerala folk lullaby, soft flute, melancholic, intimate, small room, distant temple bell"`.
  - Structure tags: `[Spoken intro][Verse][Chorus][Instrumental Break][Outro]`.
- **Local, open-source (no API cost):** heartmula (8–16GB VRAM) or Meta MusicGen (instrumental only). Good if you plan 50+ original tracks.
- **Narration (not music):** Use TTS, not music. Options (India-context): Coqui TTS (Tamil/Malayalam/Hindi models), gTTS, ElevenLabs (premium quality, pay-per-use), or local XTTS/vits for each language.

### Audio rule for Shorts
- Voiceover (TTS or human) = 60–75% of loudness.
- Music bed = 15–30%, ducked under voice.
- Never let music carry the message. If you can hum the whole short, cut the music.

## 6. Production SOP (every 3 shorts, twice the effort)

For each short, in order:
1. **Idea → Hook (15 min):** One sentence promise, verified against top 3 Shorts of the category.
2. **Script → VO (20 min):** 40–70 words spoken ≈ 25–35 seconds. Tamil/Malayalam/Hindi, simple sentence structures.
3. **Voice (15–25 min):** TTS (Coqui/gTTS/best-tier). Enrich with soft breath noise; avoid over-processed "robotic" sound; add a light reverb tail on the last line so it sits well.
4. **Visual (10–15 min):** Single concept: film frame, temple silhouette, typography, b-roll split, or AI-generated artwork. Max 3 shots per 15s (avoid jump cuts).
5. **Caption (5 min):** Burned in — keyword-first, high contrast, 2–3 lines max, one language + one English subtitle.
6. **Audio mix (5 min):** Voice up, music down, 1–1.5s fade in/out.
7. **Upload (5 min):** Title (hook + keyword), description (2 lines + 2 hashtags), tags (3–5 relevant + 1 language), custom thumbnail (text-only, 60% contrast).
8. **Schedule & repeat:** Upload same weekday, same time slot. Never skip a Friday.

## 10. Compliance & Safety (per category)

| Category | Safeguards |
|---|---|
| Cinema | Don't lift full movie dialogue as your own. Keep 5–10s clips, transform meaning, add your own edit/caps. Prefer stock + AI art first 90 days. |
| Spiritual | Reverent tone. Note "one telling" for myths. No sacrilege. |
| Language/Culture | Accurate translations; note dialect differences; credit poets/sources. Avoid stereotypes. |
| Automobile | Never give unsafe-mechanical advice ("don't tamper with the fuel pump"). Recommend a mechanic for repairs. |
| Cooking | Food safety (cool storage, don't leave rice out). Label spice level. Credit recipes. |
| AI Education | Explain hallucination clearly. Don't claim AI is human. Keep plain-language + illustrated. |
| Investments | **Educational only.** No "guaranteed returns." Add "not financial advice." Never promise high returns. Cite sources for numbers. |
| Music | Music bed must be royalty/clearance-free, or you can't sustain monetized shorts. |
